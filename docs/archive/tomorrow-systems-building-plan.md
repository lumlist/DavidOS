## FamilyAI Focus

Primary task:

AI-assisted Bark audit.

The goal is to learn whether Bark already solves the AI summaries / conversation guidance gap.

Important meta-goal:

Improve automation and parallel workflows while preserving research quality.

The Bark audit should not become a giant manual slog.

Preferred workflow:

1. Founder navigates Bark manually.
2. Founder captures screenshots and rough observations.
3. AI extracts feature inventory, trust signals, UX patterns, privacy concerns, and strategy implications.
4. Stronger model reviews the audit for blind spots.
5. Findings are converted into FamilyAI research evidence.

Manual work should focus on what only the founder can do:

- Sign up
- Navigate product
- Notice how it feels
- Capture screenshots
- Identify trust / creepiness / supportiveness
- Judge whether the product feels like surveillance or support

AI should handle:

- Feature inventory
- Screenshot interpretation
- Gap analysis
- Comparison to FamilyAI
- Strategic implications
- Follow-up questions
- Audit documentation

## DavidOS Diagnostic Loop

Begin designing a diagnostic process that keeps the workspace optimized as the work evolves.

The diagnostic should eventually answer:

1. What projects am I managing?
2. What is creating friction?
3. What tasks should be automated?
4. What agents or tools are needed?
5. What information should persist?
6. What should require approval?
7. Where am I losing context?
8. Which workflow improvement would create the most leverage next?

This may become a future service or business:

AI Workspace Diagnostic and Buildout.

Potential service promise:

I help founders, operators, and ambitious professionals diagnose their workflows and build a personalized AI operating system for their life and business.
