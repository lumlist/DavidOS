# David AI Workspace v0

## Purpose

Build a personal AI operating system that helps me manage my life, businesses, research, experiments, decisions, and agent workflows.

The system should help me become more effective across business building, personal life, health, learning, execution, and long-term planning.

## Design Principles

- AI should increase clarity, not create noise.
- Agents can collect, draft, analyze, monitor, and recommend.
- I make major decisions.
- All important decisions need rationale, evidence, confidence level, and revisit dates.
- Use stronger models for make-or-break decisions.
- Use cheaper models for drafting, organization, and routine work.
- Keep durable context in structured docs, not scattered chats.
- Automate repeatable workflows only after they are understood manually.
- Approval gates should exist for spending money, sending messages, editing production systems, creating accounts, legal/privacy claims, and major business decisions.
- The system should improve over time as AI tools and best practices evolve.

## Approval Gates

### Agents may do without approval

- Draft documents
- Summarize research
- Create checklists
- Propose plans
- Analyze uploaded files
- Identify risks
- Suggest next steps

### Agents require approval before

- Spending money
- Creating accounts
- Sending emails/messages
- Posting publicly
- Applying database migrations
- Touching production systems
- Making legal/privacy conclusions
- Changing business strategy
- Deleting files
- Committing or pushing code unless explicitly authorized
